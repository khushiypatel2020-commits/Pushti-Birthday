To My Pushtuuu ❤️

A little letter for someone very special...

Happy Birthday to one of the most special people je mari life ma unexpectedly aavi ane kyare mari life no aatlo important part bani gayi e khabar j na padi. 🥹❤️

Haju pan thodu crazy lage che jyare hu vicharu chu ke apde pehli vaar college na orientation na first day auditorium ma malya hata. E time e mane bilkul idea nahoto ke tya maleli aa chokri ek divas mari life ma aatli special bani jashe.

Aa 2–3 years ma apde ketli badhi nani-nani moments share kari che— baato, hasi-majak, random conversations, memories ane e badha moments je e time e maybe normal lagta hata, pan aaje ej moments beautiful memories bani gaya che. ❤️

Ek vastu je mane tara vishe genuinely bau game che, e che taru sambhalvu. Tara ma ek bau beautiful quality che ke tu saamena person ne genuinely heard ane understood feel karave che. Kyaarek tane kai vadhu bolvani jarur pan nathi padti; bas tu sathe hoy etlu j badhu thodu easy ane light lage che.

And honestly, hu tari aa quality ne hamesha appreciate karish. 🫶🏻

Khabar nathi hu aa tane enough vaar kahu chu ke nahi, but I am genuinely so grateful ke apni paths cross thai. Aatla badha loko mathi apde ek-bijane malya—and honestly, I am really glad that we did. ❤️

Thank you mari college life ni aatli badhi memories no part banva mate. Thank you badhi laughs, conversations, nani-nani moments ane simply tu jevi che evi j reh je hamesha mate. Mari ek savthi favourite memories aapdi scooty ride rehse bhale ne e khali college thi famous vada pav sudhi ni j kem naa hoi but i will never forget that. 🥹

"Here's to you, to our memories & to many more beautiful moments." ❤️

Have jyare tu tari life no ek bijo beautiful year start kari rahi che, I just hope ke aa year tara mate ghani badhi happiness ane smile karvana reasons lai ne aave. ✨

I hope ke tu je pan dreams jue che, e badha slowly-slowly reality bane. Tane hamesha eva loko male je tane genuinely value kare, ane tu aatli badhi beautiful memories create kare ke future ma ene yaad kari ne tara face par automatically smile aavi jaay. ❤️

Ane hu bas etlu j hope karu chu ke life game etli change thai jaay, pan apni life ma hamesha ek nano corner e badhi memories mate reserved rahe je apde sathe create kari che. 🫶🏻

Happy Birthday once again, Pushtuuu! 🎂✨

Hamesha aavi j beautiful soul rehje, hamesha smile karti rehje, ane je amazing listener ane friend tu che, e hamesha rehje. Ane kyarey bhulti nahi ke tu ketli loved ane appreciated che. ❤️

With lots of love,
Khushi 💗
